# ACADEMIX 2.0 — Official Transcripts Reference

## Template & Output Structure (NEW HIGH SCHOOL TRANSCRIPT 26-27)
1. **Header:** Institutional metadata dynamically retrieved from `system_settings`.
2. **Student Information:** Full name, STU code, date of birth, address, phone, graduation status.
3. **Academic History:** Grouped multi-annually by Academic Year and Grade Level.
   - Course Title
   - Credits Earned (e.g. 1.00)
   - Final Grade (0-100)
   - GPA Points (4.00 Scale)
4. **Credits & GPA Summary:**
   - Total Credits Earned (Cumulative)
   - Cumulative GPA
   - Class Rank / Honors
5. **List of Other Schools Attended:** Transferred credits, dates, and previous school names.
6. **Graduation Summary:** Diploma Earned (Standard High School Diploma), Graduation Date.
7. **Certification & School Seal:** Ready for official signature, registrar stamp, and sealing.
